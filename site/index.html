Meet our ambassador

Tricia Downing

Wheelchair Division Athlete Ambassador

In 2025, Tricia made history as the first wheelchair athlete to compete in the Santa Fe International Half Marathon — and came back in 2026 to make sure this race belongs to athletes of every ability.

After a cycling accident left her paralyzed from the chest down, Tricia found her way back through adaptive sports — and then kept going. She was the first female wheelchair athlete to complete an Iron-distance triathlon. These days she's a Paralympic shooter, training with her sights set on representing the United States at the 2028 Los Angeles Paralympic Games.

She's also an author, keynote speaker, disability advocate, and bicultural adoptee. Her book, Cycle of Hope: A Journey from Paralysis to Possibility, tells the whole story better than we can here.

Racing in the high desert

Respect the Altitude

Santa Fe sits higher than Denver. The thin air is part of the challenge — and the charm. A little planning goes a long way.

Arrive early if you can

Coming from low elevation? Two to three days in town before the race helps your body adjust — and Santa Fe is a fine place to spend them.

Hydrate all week

The high desert is dry and you lose more fluid than you notice. Keep a water bottle in hand for a few days before the gun.

Pace the climb to the turnaround

The long grade up the River Trail tops out at 6,883 ft at the mile-9 turnaround. Run it a touch slower than goal pace — the downhill run-in to the farm is where you make it up.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

The short answers, all in one place. Everything here is covered in more detail up the page.

When and where does the race start?

Sunday, September 19, 2027, at 7:30 AM Mountain time, from Romero Park in Agua Fria Village. The Half Marathon and the 3-Amigos Relay start together and finish at Reunity Resources Farm on the Santa Fe River corridor.

How long is the course, really?

13.1 GPS-measured miles (this course isn't USATF-certified yet). You start at 6,640 ft, dip to 6,567 ft near mile 4, climb to the 6,883 ft turnaround at mile 9, and finish at 6,590 ft — about 651 ft of total climb. See the full elevation profile.

What's the route like?

About the first five miles run city streets out of Agua Fria Village — after that you're on the car-free Santa Fe River Trail the rest of the way, up to the mile-9 turnaround and back down to the farm. New course for 2027: no highway shoulders, and most of your miles are traffic-free.

Is there a time limit?

Yes — 4 hours 30 minutes from the 7:30 AM start. Course support ends at 12:00 PM.

Where do I pick up my packet?

At Reunity Resources Farm, for all events: Friday 10 AM–6 PM and Saturday 10 AM–5 PM. 5K and 10K bibs are mailed about a week before race day — collect your shirt at Reunity. Sunday race-morning pickup (Half & Relay) is for emergencies only, 6–7 AM at the start.

What's the 3-Amigos Relay?

The three-person team event run alongside the half marathon, starting with it at 7:30 AM Sunday. Leg details and team registration are on RunSignup.

Are there shorter races?

Yes — Saturday brings the 5K, the 10K, and the Kids Fun Dash at Reunity Resources Farm. Start times will be announced soon; bibs for the 5K and 10K are mailed ahead of race weekend.

How do I register?

On RunSignup — registration is open now.

How do I find my time after I finish?

Chip times post live as you cross the line. Open the live leaderboard and type your bib number into "Find my time" — past editions are on RunSignup results.

Will there be race photos?

Yes, free ones — every year. Links to all the galleries are in the Photos section.

I live at sea level. How bad is the altitude?

You'll feel it — Santa Fe sits higher than Denver. Arrive a couple of days early if you can, hydrate all week, and pace the climb to the mile-9 turnaround gently. Full advice in Respect the Altitude.

Can my business have a booth?

Yes — one flat $300 fee covers both days of packet-pickup weekend at Reunity Resources Farm. See Vendor Booths.
