Race Info

Everything you need for race weekend — where it starts, where it ends, and how to get your bib.

Start — Agua Fria Village

Romero Park, Agua Fria Village, elevation 6,640 ft.

About the first five miles run city streets, then it's the car-free Santa Fe River Trail. Gun at 7:30 AM MDT — mornings up here are crisp, so dress for the start, not the finish.

Finish — Reunity Resources Farm

Reunity Resources Farm on the Santa Fe River corridor, elevation 6,590 ft.

Course cutoff is 4 hours 30 minutes — course support ends at 12:00 PM. Live results post as you cross the line.

Packet Pickup

All events pick up at Reunity Resources Farm:
Friday 10 AM–6 PM and Saturday 10 AM–5 PM.

5K and 10K bibs are mailed about a week before race day (collect shirts at Reunity). Sunday race-morning pickup (Half & Relay) is for emergencies only, 6–7 AM.
